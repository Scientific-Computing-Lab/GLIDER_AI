# Confirmatory downstream-response liquid-data audit

Audit completed 19 August 2026 before any new downstream QM reference or
model score was evaluated. The existing six-solute, 24-case held-out-W4
experiment remains unchanged on commit `e83ec611a03ba7cfb1578ec1dc112713af641ed4`;
its frozen protocol is commit `db534143`.

## Verdict

The workspace contains exactly ten genuinely new neutral solute identities
with completed explicit-water equilibration states that support the same
W1--W12 construction. This meets, but does not exceed, the requested minimum
for a confirmatory panel. One configuration per solute is used. No outcome,
response value, or model prediction entered the audit or selection.

The ten solutes are 2OJ9-original, cdk2-1h1q, cdk2-28, ethane, ethanol,
jnk1-17124, jnk1-18631, methane, tyk2-ejm_42, and tyk2-ejm_45. All are neutral;
their elements are a subset of H/C/N/O/S/Cl/Br, which is within the frozen
GLIDER/MACE-POLAR element support. None is an identity match to any of the 14
chemistries used to train the released GLIDER response head. Exact structures,
source commits, paths, hashes, equilibration-state times, W1--W12 indices, and
distances are recorded in `../configurations/configurations.csv`.

The panel comprises five broader source families: 2OJ9, cdk2, small
aliphatics, jnk1, and tyk2. Thus, ten solutes—not ten unrelated scaffold
families—are the independent statistical units. Paired analogues in the cdk2,
jnk1, and tyk2 groups will remain visible in raw and family sensitivity
summaries; they are not silently treated as evidence of ten maximally diverse
chemical families.

## Search scope

The audit searched the complete workspace for PDB/XYZ/NPZ/NetCDF/DCD/XTC/TRR/
HDF5/restart artifacts, all `initial_solvated.pdb`, `aqueous.npz`, and
trajectory-like names, and every Git root under
`/home/galoren/freesolv_agent_benchmark`. Histories and all reachable branches
were inspected for the GLIDER worktrees, `HYDRATION_STAGE1`, Transformato and
Transformato-systems, the FEP/MD tools, the foundation solvation repositories,
and the method-agnostic/route-C repositories.

The authoritative GLIDER/liquid-bridge and Hamiltonian-lift sources contain
only the original six pilot solutes. Current Transformato-systems files add
nine pre-optimized starting boxes but no completed state for those named
solutes; its alchemical restart directories are methane copies. The six AQUA
boxes were generated by insertion into a compact water template and were not
sampled from liquid trajectories. Designed hydration/many-body sets stop at
finite one-to-four-water clusters. External test data are biomolecular,
water-only, or duplicates.

The usable new states were recovered from the history of
`https://github.com/cbc-univie/transformato.git`. They are OpenMM XML states
from the completed `step4_equilibration` stage at 25 ps (ethane and ethanol)
or 125 ps (the other eight). The exact source commits range from 2020 to 2022
and are pinned in the configuration registry. Parent salt ions and CHARMM
halogen lone-pair virtual sites, where present, are excluded before the
solute/water ranking; no such site enters a QM or model input.

## Selection and exclusions

All distinct eligible completed states are retained. Repeated 2OJ9 test/mod
aliases and the alternate tautomer are collapsed to one solute identity; the
lexicographically canonical non-test `2OJ9-original` state is used. Starting
boxes, failed attempts, alchemical intermediates, proteins, host--guest
systems, water-only examples, and configurations lacking W4--W12 are excluded
without consulting any downstream outcome. The machine-readable source-group
accounting is `workspace_liquid_audit.csv`.
