# MACE-POLAR-1-L · Panel II

The paper’s response-unfitted polar baseline for spatial response ESP. The files here are archived predictions for the fixed 96 configurations in Panel II; they are not training labels. Use the [method-group guide](../README.md) and the panel configuration registry to align records. Model and reference data were separated at the prediction freeze.

The [paper-to-data map](../../../../results/README.md) identifies the camera-ready observable and comparison. Other methods do not receive a proxy score for an observable they do not provide.
